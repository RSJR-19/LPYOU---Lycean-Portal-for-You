Services you may need

Find useful campus resources and support services in one place.

Explore the offices and resources available to the LPU Cavite community.

LIBRARY


LIBRARY

The Academic Resource Center (ARC) supports learning and research with library resources, online references, and study spaces for students, faculty, and staff.

Visit the ARC to explore its learning resources and facilities. Check the University website for current services, hours, and access information.

Learn more

REGISTRAR


REGISTRAR

The Registrar's Office maintains student academic records and assists with enrollment-related transactions. Services include registration, transcript requests, certifications, and verification of academic information.

Contact the Registrar's Office for help with academic documents, enrollment questions, and other student-record concerns.

Learn more

IT SUPPORT


IT SUPPORT

The Center for Information Technology and Digital Learning (ITD) oversees the University's technology systems, data, networks, and telecommunications.

ITD supports the University's digital services and the systems used for campus transactions.

Its support includes help-desk assistance, ID-related services, network and telecommunications support, and computer laboratory maintenance.

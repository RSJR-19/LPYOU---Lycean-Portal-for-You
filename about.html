ABOUT US

A student-made guide to campus life

LPYOU brings helpful campus information together so Lyceans can find updates, services, and contacts more easily.

Our Mission

Make campus information easier to find by bringing announcements, useful services, and important contacts together in one student-friendly portal.

Our Vision

A connected LPU Cavite community where students can quickly discover the information and resources they need.

Our Purpose

LPYOU is made by Lyceans as a convenient guide. For time-sensitive announcements and official requirements, always confirm details with the relevant LPU Cavite office.

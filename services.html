LIBRARY

REGISTRAR


REGISTRAR

The Registrar's Office is responsible for maintaining student academic records and managing enrollment-related transactions. It provides services such as registration, transcript requests, certification of records, and verification of academic information to ensure accurate and organized student documentation.

The Registrar's Office also assists students with academic documents, enrollment concerns, and other record-related inquiries. It ensures that student information is properly managed, updated, and accessible to authorized individuals.

IT SUPPORT


IT SUPPORT

The Center for Information Technology and Digital Learning (ITD) holds the principal responsibility for all ITD-related projects of the University. It is mainly in charge of systems, data, network, and telecommunications administration.

The ITD provides solution for various transaction processes. It focuses on any several aspects of development such as generation, evaluation and administration of automated system being utilized.

ITD also provides extensive ITD service like help-desk support (end user assistance, ID production, validation, network, and telecommunication, etc.) and the computer laboratory maintenance.
